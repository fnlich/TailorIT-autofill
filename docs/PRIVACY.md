# Privacy: what the extension keeps, where, and why it asks for each permission

TailorIT Autofill fills job applications from a profile the person keeps in their own TailorIT. It never submits an application, never logs a field value, and keeps the resume only for the seconds it takes to attach it. This file is the source for the store listing's privacy answers and permission justifications.

## Data inventory

| Data | Where | How long |
|---|---|---|
| The bearer token that connects the extension to TailorIT | the extension origin's IndexedDB (a content script cannot open it) and the service worker's memory | until Disconnect, its 90-day expiry, or a 401 |
| The profile | the service worker's memory only | the run; dropped after 5 idle minutes. The sensitive part (work authorization, self-identification, age, pronouns, pay) is fetched only when a form asks such a question, and only if the person allowed it when connecting |
| Saved answers | the service worker's memory, fetched with the profile | the run; dropped with the profile. An answer the person chooses to save (Save my answer, offered only after they fixed a question the extension left for them) is read from the page and sent straight to their TailorIT, never kept by the extension. Never for self-identification, legal, pay, consent or attention-check questions, nor for one whose label names no topic but whose hint, section heading or options do, nor for an answer that is itself a self-identification answer or a decline ("Woman", "Prefer not to say") |
| The resume file | the service worker, then the page's file input | seconds: released once the input is set and verified, or within 60 s |
| Settings | `chrome.storage.local`, restricted to the extension's own contexts | until changed |
| Run progress (RunState): keys, hashes, counters, task ids, checked canaries | `chrome.storage.local` | 24 hours after its last update; at most 20 kept, each at most 256 KiB with a capped number of entries (one per step of a form, at most 30 steps), so together they never take more than half of the browser's 10 MiB allowance. It never holds a field value, nor a hash of one. The AI drafts written (waiting for the person, or accepted) are kept by their field keys only, hashed as below, so a page the browser reloads with a draft in place still shows it as an AI draft. The AI's answers are not kept here: the content script remembers, in memory only for the page's life, each answer's keyed value hash (a MAC under a key it never stores), so a later Fill on the same page rows a field still holding one as the AI's answer. Which answer revealed which question (the reveal edges) is kept by field keys whose label and section heading text are each replaced by a hash (the first 16 hex characters of its SHA-256), since a page can show a typed answer back in a question's label or a heading ("Preferred name if not …") |
| Review reports: statuses, labels, reasons, and a multi-step form's progress line ("Step 2 of 4") | `chrome.storage.session` (memory) | until the tab closes, the page that sent it is gone (its frame loads another page or the extension finds it gone), or the browser restarts. Never a value or an option label. A row's label and the progress line are the page's own text, kept as the page shows them so the person can find the question, by design: they stay in memory, and a page that shows a typed answer back in a label shows it there too. With the panel on the page chosen, a tab's reports go to the one frame the person opened that panel in, and only while it is open |
| Which frame of a tab shows the panel on the page: its frame id and document id | `chrome.storage.session` | until the panel closes, that page is gone, the tab closes or the browser restarts |
| Registered frames: origin, platform, document id, flags, and how many times a single-page application changed the frame's address (a count) | `chrome.storage.session` | until the tab closes or the extension finds the frame's page gone. Never a full page address |
| Runs: the tab, frame and document a fill was started in, its application key (a hash, never the address), whether it is a dry run, and whether the sensitive part could be read for it | `chrome.storage.session` | until the tab closes, the frame starts another fill, the page it was started in is gone, or the browser restarts; at most 50 kept. Never a value |
| Posting policy notices: the ids of the "do not use AI" patterns a posting page matched, and whether one was read and clear, with the order they were last used in (application keys only) | `chrome.storage.session` | until the browser restarts; at most 200 postings, the least recently used going first. Never the posting's text |
| A pending connection: a single-use random state, while TailorIT's Connect page is open | `chrome.storage.session` | 10 minutes, or its first use |
| The AI questions' task ids: a name for each batch of questions sent to TailorIT's AI (a hash of its questions' keys and options, page text only, and for a pick of the profile's revision number in place of its values), and the id TailorIT gave its task, by application key | `chrome.storage.session`, and the application's RunState in `chrome.storage.local` (the same names and ids) | in `chrome.storage.session`, an hour after each was noted (TailorIT deletes the task then) and until the browser restarts, at most 720 kept; in RunState, as long as the RunState: 24 hours after its last update, at most 720 per application. Never a question, an option, an answer or a profile value, and no hash of a profile value |
| A fill's AI questions and answers: the fields waiting for the AI, the page text they were asked with, and what the AI answered | the service worker's memory only | until the fill is replaced or its tab closes, or the worker stops |
| The TailorIT account's name and email, to show "Connected as …" | the service worker's memory only | until the connection changes or the worker stops |
| What TailorIT knows of the open posting at a fill (the job, its saved posting text, the resume it suggests, the person's tracker entry for it and the resume's keyword match) | the service worker's memory only | until the fill is replaced or its tab closes, the connection changes, or the worker stops. The posting text is read only by the "do not use AI" check and never reaches an AI model |
| The side panel's job card (v2): the job's title, company, location, pay, arrangement and posting date, the tracker entry's status, the resume a fill attaches (its title, which may be the person's name, its file kind and why it was chosen) and its keyword match. Never the posting text, an id, the page's address or a file name | the service worker's memory, per page (its tab, frame and document); a view of it in the side panel while the panel shows that tab | 10 minutes after it was read, or until that page is gone (or a single-page application moves it to another address), its tab closes, the connection or the tab's resume choice changes, or the worker stops; the side panel's view until it follows another tab, the connection changes or it closes. Never in storage, a log or the page's own process: the form's frame relays the request, so TailorIT reads the page's address from Chrome, and the frame learns nothing of the answer. TailorIT is asked for it only while the side panel shows a tab whose form was found, when the person adds or saves the job or changes the resume, and at Autofill or Dry run (whose own lookup becomes the card); never on a page load by itself. TailorIT keeps no page address, except that Add this job to TailorIT stores the posting in the person's TailorIT jobs, as any job there |
| The resume chosen for a tab with Change (v2): its id | the service worker's memory only | until the tab closes, the person chooses again, the connection changes, or the worker stops |
| The resume list behind Change (v2): each resume's id, title, file kind and whether it was built or uploaded | the side panel's memory | while the picker is open |
| A custom question and its answer (v2, "Ask a custom question") | the side panel's memory; the service worker holds them only for the call | until the panel closes or follows another tab. Never stored or logged |
| Accepted option matches: the chosen option's normalized label for a question's options and the value it stood for (a cache key), kept only when the person submitted the form with that field as the extension left it | `chrome.storage.session` | until the browser restarts; at most 5,000, the least recently used going first. Never for AI picks, self-identification, legal, consent or pay questions |
| Which rows each fill has counted (the anonymous counters' bookkeeping): the rows' field keys, as the review reports hold them | `chrome.storage.session` (`telemetry:counted`), only while the person opts in | until the counters are cleared or the browser restarts; the latest 20 fills, at most 2,000 keys each. Never a value |
| Anonymous counters | `chrome.storage.local` (`telemetry:v1`), only when the person opts in (off by default) | until cleared on the options page; opting out stops the counting. Exactly five fields and a count per combination of them: platform, field kind, matcher stage, success or fail, whether the page addressed AI tools. No key, label, address, account, install id, time or version. Nothing is sent anywhere: the options page shows them and saves them as a file only when the person asks |

Nothing is logged except closed codes with numbers or flags (`src/shared/log.ts`: its types make it impossible to log a string a page or a profile supplied).

## What reaches an AI model

Only through the person's own TailorIT, which runs the Claude CLI, and only when the AI settings allow it:
- the visible text of a question and its visible options, to pick among them or to say what a question asks;
- for a pick, the profile's value for that question (a school, a degree, a field of study, a language, a referral source, or a custom answer). A value that names a sensitive topic or a sum is never sent, except that a school's, a degree's or a field of study's own name is checked for a sum only, since "Gender Studies" or "United States Military Academy" is just its name;
- for a draft (on by default since the owner's amendments, docs/SPEC-AMENDMENTS.md), the visible question, the posting's visible text on the form's own page (none for a form embedded in another site's page, and none in a dry run, which asks for no draft), the job's title and company, the words hidden page text asked AI tools to write (so TailorIT refuses a draft holding one), and which resume to draw on. TailorIT adds facts from the resume itself. A draft is written into the form, marked as the AI's on the page and in the review (it waits for the person's confirmation only while the "Wait for me to confirm each draft" setting is on). No draft is asked for a box in a self-identification, legal, pay or consent context: under such a heading, revealed by such a question, following one up ("If yes, please explain"), or in a section of such questions only;
- for an AI answer (on by default since the owner's amendments), the same as a draft's, for a question nothing else settled: its visible label, hint, section and, for a choice, its visible options' clean labels by their places. TailorIT reads the facts itself from the resume and the profile's non-sensitive part (work history, education, languages, skills, job preferences), never the sensitive part, contact details, the name, the address or saved answers. An answer with nothing to go on is written marked "AI guess". The same screens keep every self-identification, legal, criminal-history, pay, consent and attention-check question, and every box in such a context, from it, and so does a question about another person (an emergency contact, a referee, a manager or a spouse), an identifier (a number, an ID, a licence, a passport), a postal address, a security clearance, or a marketing or contact consent;
- for Edit with AI (the person's click on the button in a text box), the box's question and what it holds now (page text, which may be the person's own words), with the draft's context, so TailorIT rewrites it. A box whose text holds words hidden page text asked AI tools to write, names a sensitive topic or a sum, holds a self-identification answer or a decline ("Prefer not to say") on a line of its own, or holds an email address, a phone number, a link or a postal address, is never sent (the extension's screen, as TailorIT screens the same text).

- for a custom question the person types in the side panel (v2), their question, the posting's visible text on the form's own page (none for a form inside another site's page), the job's title and company, the words hidden page text asked AI tools to write, and which resume to draw on (the one the job card attaches, or the one chosen with Change); TailorIT adds facts from that resume. A question about self-identification, work authorization, sponsorship, pay or consent, or one that checks the person read the form, is never sent, by the extension and again by TailorIT.

Edit with AI and the custom question run only when the person asks (their click on Edit with AI, or Get answer), and only while AI-written answers (the "Draft open answers" setting) are on: with it off, Edit with AI is not offered, the custom question box is disabled with the reason, and the worker refuses either if asked. A custom question still works on a posting that says AI is not allowed, since the person asked; the side panel shows that posting's notice above it, and with the answer.

Never: self-identification (EEO) answers, work authorization, sponsorship, criminal record, pay, consent, contact details, hidden page text, or TailorIT's stored copy of a posting. Questions that look like self-identification, legal, pay or consent questions, by their words, their hint, their section's heading or their options, are screened out before classification, a draft or an answer, by the extension and again by TailorIT.

When a posting says AI-assisted applications are not allowed, or its form asks the person to confirm they did not use AI, the default ("Fill from my profile only", the owner's amendments) sends nothing at all to the AI for that run: no pick, classification, draft or answer, and Edit with AI is not offered. A run that learns it only on a later pass gives up what it was still waiting for from the AI and writes none of it, and the worker asks the AI nothing more for that run. The other choices are "Ask me each time" (the policy card) and "Use AI anyway".

## Permissions

| Key | Value | Why | Not used instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| `permissions` | `storage` | Settings, a run's progress across page reloads (no field values), and the review report of open tabs | `unlimitedStorage`, `cookies` |
| | `sidePanel` | The review panel: what was filled, from where, and what needs the person | a popup, which closes on any click in the page |
| `host_permissions` | the ATS hosts (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday) | Exactly the sites whose application forms are filled; also lets the panel see the active tab's address on those sites without the `tabs` permission, and the service worker read a form tab's address now from Chrome's description of the message's sender (a single-page application's next posting, which the frame's own sender address does not follow). That address is used for the lookup at once and never kept | `<all_urls>`; `activeTab`, which is revoked on navigation and so cannot follow a multi-step application |
| | `http://localhost:3100/*` | The service worker calls the person's own TailorIT with its token; TailorIT sends no CORS headers by design | CORS on TailorIT |
| `externally_connectable` | `http://localhost:3100/*` | TailorIT's Connect page hands over a one-time code after the person clicks Connect | the typed code (kept as the fallback) |
| `action` | a title, and TailorIT's mark at 16 and 32 px (`default_icon`) | The toolbar button opens the panel | none |
| `icons` | TailorIT's mark at 16, 32, 48 and 128 px, PNGs packaged with the extension (`public/icon`) | The extensions page and the store show it; nothing is fetched, and the files are not web-accessible | remote images |
| `side_panel`, `options_ui` | the panel and settings pages | none | none |
| Absent by design | `tabs`, `scripting`, `activeTab`, `cookies`, `webRequest`, `debugger`, `alarms`, `offscreen`, `downloads`, `web_accessible_resources`, a CSP override, remote code | | |

Development builds alone add, for saving page snapshots as test fixtures ([FIXTURES.md](FIXTURES.md)):
- the `pageCapture` permission;
- two keyboard commands: Alt+Shift+S saves the page in the active tab, every frame included, and Alt+Shift+D does so after 3 seconds;
- the page that saves the snapshot. It offers the file as a link to save, so the snapshot never leaves the computer, and it lets go of it when its tab closes.

`scripts/check-build.mjs` fails any build it checks (Chrome, Edge and the e2e build, all production mode) that holds the permission, a `commands` key, the capture page or the hotkeys' code.

## First-run disclosure (shown before Connect)

The options page shows this above Connect whenever the extension is not connected. Its words live in `src/shared/copy.ts` (`COPY.options.connection`), and `src/shared/copy.test.ts` keeps this copy the same. It is a draft for the owner's review: it names the AI help as on, because picking from lists and working out unusual questions are on by default.

> **Before you connect**
>
> TailorIT Autofill fills job applications on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday from the profile you keep in your own TailorIT. It reads the job pages you open on those sites, fills what it is sure of and lists the rest for you to review. It never submits an application: you do.
>
> While its side panel shows an application page, it tells your TailorIT which job page you are on, so TailorIT can name the job, your tracker entry for it and your match; it does so again when you choose Autofill or Dry run, add the job or change the resume. TailorIT does not keep the page's address unless you add the job. When you choose Autofill, it fetches your profile from TailorIT, and your resume when the form asks for one. It keeps them in memory only, never on disk: your profile until five minutes after its last use, and the resume only for the seconds it takes to attach it. This browser keeps your settings and notes on each application's progress, never your answers.
>
> Its AI help sends the visible text of a question and its visible options, with the answer from your profile it is trying to match, to your TailorIT, which asks Claude. Picking from lists, working out unusual questions, drafting open answers and answering the questions your profile does not are on unless you turn them off below; drafts and answers also give Claude the posting's visible text, and TailorIT gives it your resume and profile to write from. An answer with nothing to go on is marked "AI guess". On a posting that says AI is not allowed, nothing goes to the AI unless you choose otherwise below, or ask a question of your own in the side panel. That question, and Edit with AI in a text box, go to the AI only when you ask, and only while AI-written answers (Draft open answers, below) are on. Your self-identification, work authorization, sponsorship, criminal record, pay and consent answers never go to the AI. The extension talks only to your TailorIT.
>
> Choosing Connect means you agree to this.
