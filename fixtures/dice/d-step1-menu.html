Summary

  • Lead and mentor the Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) team.
  • Ensure high availability, scalability, and performance of enterprise web applications.
  • Monitor application health, infrastructure, and production environments.
  • Lead production incident management, troubleshooting, and root cause analysis (RCA).
  • Define and implement SLIs, SLOs, and SLAs.
  • Identify application performance bottlenecks and implement reliability improvements.
  • Work closely with development, DevOps, QA, and infrastructure teams.
  • Build automation solutions to reduce manual operational tasks.
  • Establish proactive monitoring, alerting, and observability practices.
  • Manage production releases, deployments, and rollback strategies.
