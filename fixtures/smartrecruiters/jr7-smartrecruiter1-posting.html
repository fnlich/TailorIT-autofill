Infrastructure & LiveOps Engineer

  • Full-time

Company Description

Unchained Entertainment is an independent video game development studio founded in March 2011. We are currently developing Camelot Unchained, a niche, RvR-focused, old-school MMORPG inspired by large-scale realm warfare and player-driven worlds.

Job Description

We are looking for an Infrastructure & LiveOps Engineer to help support and maintain the overall game system infrastructure and live service health of Camelot Unchained. 

Responsibilities

  • Own continuous integration and deployment pipeline.  Add functionality, investigate broken or stalled jobs, improve and extend automation.

  • Own LiveOps monitoring and alerting.  Ensure health coverage on critical systems, maintain and refine alerts.

  • Own Infrastructure as Code, provisioning and maintaining cloud infrastructure to support team efforts.

  • Support bug, performance, and crash investigations by auditing crash dumps, logs, and metrics.

Qualifications

Requirements

  • Familiarity with Linux and Windows administration

  • Experience customizing and deploying container images to multiple environments

  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, bonus points for Jenkins

  • Experience with source control, bonus points for GitLab

  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools and techniques, bonus points for Terraform

  • Familiarity with C#, C++, Powershell, and JS

  • Experience with metrics servers, bonus points for Graphite, Grafana, and Prometheus

  • Experience with cloud services, bonus points for GCP

Bonus

  • Experience with MongoDB

  • Experience with PostgreSQL

  • Experience with Zookeeper

  • Experience with Etcd

  • Experience with certificate management

  • Experience with managing VPNs and Strongswan

  • Experience with Active Directory
