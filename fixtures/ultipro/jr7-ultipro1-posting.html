    What this position entails

    The Senior Staff REDACTED will play a pivotal role in shaping the future of our organization by driving early-stage innovation and zero-to-one development of new products and capabilities. As a key member of our R&D team, this role focuses on transforming ideas into working solutions - rapidly developing MVPs, testing concepts, and iterating based on real user feedback. Working across ambiguous problem spaces, you’ll translate emerging opportunities and evolving business needs into solutions that help us quickly determine what delivers value.

    Operating in a dynamic, experimental environment, this role balances strategic thinking with hands-on execution. It requires navigating ambiguity, continuously testing assumptions, and using rapid feedback loops to refine ideas and uncover scalable opportunities. The position plays a critical role in identifying product-market fit and shaping new offerings - helping define the next generation of solutions that will drive long-term growth. As a senior technical leader, you will take a realistic approach that keeps business needs, security requirements, architectural vision, and end-user expectations in mind, set technical direction for the team, and effectively communicate with business stakeholders while collaborating across functional teams.

    What you will be doing

    • Lead the design, development, and iteration of MVPs, prototypes, and early-stage solutions to explore new product and capability opportunities
    • Rapidly test ideas by building lightweight implementations and getting them in front of users for feedback
    • Translate ambiguous problem spaces and emerging needs into tangible technical solutions
    • Continuously refine concepts through experimentation, data, and user insights to determine what delivers value
    • Build and deploy solutions primarily within AWS, leveraging cloud-native services to enable rapid development and iteration
    • Experiment with AI-driven approaches both as a tool for faster delivery and as a core component of new product features
    • Provide technical leadership within a small, cross-functional team to shape and validate new ideas and approaches
    • Partner with technical and non-technical stakeholders across business units to gather feedback, validate assumptions, and ensure focus on solving the most valuable problems
    • Balance speed and quality by applying appropriate engineering practices for early-stage development
    • Drive technical decisions and architectural direction that enable fast iteration while supporting future scalability, building scalable solutions and infrastructure
    • Support deployment of prototypes and early solutions to enable real-world testing and learning
    • Set best practices for R&D that enable us to deliver software that is easily adopted by the organization upon handoff
    • Responsibly self-manage workload given project priorities, deadlines, and deliverables, and help guide the priorities of others on the team
    • Serve as a subject matter expert and mentor, educating and consulting with internal teams on new and emerging technologies, including AI-driven development approaches
