The Role

As the Assistant General Manager, you will assist with all aspects of a site, including developing staffing plans, recruitment and hiring, training, vendor coordination, overseeing daily operational flow, driving revenue growth, financial accountability, and all other factors of the operation, ensuring a best-in-class Guest experience.


This role reports to the General Manager and is based in King of Prussia, PA.


What you will do: 

  • Assist the General Manager with the overall on-site operations for a Netflix House location, ensuring operating within budget constraints.
  • Develop hiring strategies for directly managed operations teams, as well as guiding third-party vendors, to ensure operational coverage and service quality.
  • Optimize ongoing operations to achieve maximum efficiency and best-in-class Guest Service.
  • Directly manage F&B vendors, coordinating with them across all operational aspects.
  • Align with key stakeholders across Live Experiences, consumer products, finance & strategy, legal, human resources, and content teams.
  • Promote and foster a mindset of continuous innovation for internal teams and external partners.
  • Foster a safety-focused culture by participating in the safety committee and collaborating with the Safety and EHS team

What we need from you:

  • Applicants must be at least 18 years of age to be considered for employment.
  • Demonstrated 5+ years of operations management experience ideally in the themed entertainment, retail, restaurant, or hospitality industries. . 
  • Strong knowledge of hiring practices and training & development strategies.
  • Experience managing direct and indirect teams in an environment that embraces continual learning and change.
  • Drive a safe, efficient, and guest-centric Netflix House by ensuring EHS compliance, operational excellence, and vendor safety while fostering innovation and profitability.
  • Excellent communication skills, both verbal and digital.
  • Excellent leadership skills.
  • Strong desire to think outside the box and innovate.
  • Strong financial acumen.
