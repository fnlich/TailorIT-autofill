Position Summary

The Azure Platform Engineer builds and supports the Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) environments, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, and automation that form the internal platform our engineering teams use to deliver software reliably and securely. Working hands-on with AKS, Azure, Azure DevOps, Azure Bicep, and application and transport-layer networking (OSI layers 3–7), you will partner with software development, operations, and quality assurance teams to improve continuous integration, continuous delivery, infrastructure automation, and release reliability. You will work under the guidance of senior engineers while taking ownership of well-defined projects and tasks.

 

In this role, you will help build and run the AKS-based platform that supports reliable product delivery, scalable cloud infrastructure, and consistent, self-service deployment standards across engineering teams, while growing your technical skills.

 

Job Duties:

  • Deploy, operate, and troubleshoot workloads on Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS), including cluster configuration, scaling, upgrades, and ingress, and escalate complex issues as needed.
  • Maintain platform tooling and processes across AKS, Azure, Azure DevOps, and Azure Bicep, collaborating with software development, operations, and quality assurance teams to streamline continuous integration, continuous delivery, and automation, with guidance from senior team members.
  • Develop and maintain infrastructure as code (IaC) using Azure Bicep to provision and manage cloud resources in Azure, supporting scalability, performance, and security.
  • Build, maintain, and improve Azure DevOps pipelines for building, testing, and deploying applications, including pipeline configuration, integrations, and security.
  • Apply secure DevOps practices, including secrets management, least-privilege access, and vulnerability scanning in pipelines and infrastructure, in support of security and HIPAA compliance requirements.
  • Support and troubleshoot application and network traffic at OSI layers 4–7, including TCP/UDP load balancing, TLS/SSL certificates, HTTP/HTTPS routing, DNS, and Kubernetes ingress, working with Azure networking services such as Azure Load Balancer and Application Gateway, and escalating complex issues to senior engineers.
  • Configure and use monitoring, logging, and alerting tools (such as Datadog, Azure Monitor, and Log Analytics) and participate in the on-call rotation to respond to and resolve production incidents and pipeline or infrastructure issues.
  • Participate in code and pipeline reviews and learn from senior engineers to build technical skills and follow best practices.
  • Build and document reusable platform components, such as pipeline templates, Bicep modules, and Helm charts, that give development teams self-service deployment capabilities on AKS.
  • All other job-related duties as assigned



Minimum Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in a related field, or equivalent practical experience, with 2+ years of DevOps, cloud, or infrastructure experience, including Azure provisioning, automation, access controls, and production support.
  • Experience with Git, including branching, merging, and resolving merge conflicts.
  • Hands-on experience with Azure DevOps pipelines, including pipeline creation, configuration, integration, and release automation.
  • Working experience with Azure Bicep (or similar IaC tools such as Terraform or ARM templates) for provisioning and managing Azure resources.
  • Knowledge of scripting and automation using Python, PowerShell, or other relevant technologies.
  • Hands-on experience deploying and operating workloads on Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) or Kubernetes, with Docker and containerization.
  • Working knowledge of networking at OSI layers 4–7, including TCP/UDP, TLS/SSL, HTTP/HTTPS, DNS, load balancing, and reverse proxies.
  • Strong problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills.
  • Willingness to participate in and contribute to the team's on-call rotation, including occasional after-hours support.



Preferred Skills and Experience

  • Experience with Helm and AKS cluster operations such as node pools, upgrades, and autoscaling (ideal candidate)
  • Experience with Azure networking and traffic management services (Application Gateway, Load Balancer, Front Door, Azure Firewall), Kubernetes ingress controllers, or service mesh
  • Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Azure Monitor, Log Analytics, Grafana, or Prometheus
  • Familiarity with secure DevOps practices (secrets management, least-privilege access, vulnerability scanning) and HIPAA or other compliance requirements in a healthcare environment
  • Azure (e.g., AZ-104, AZ-400, AZ-700) or Kubernetes (e.g., CKA, CKAD) certifications are a plus.
